01 / Biography & Trajectory

It may not be obvious, but Miguel studied architecture in Madrid, with a minor in semiotics. Preferring billboards to blueprints, he walked away from a promising studio career to study Communication Design at Central Saint Martins in London.

Over the last fourteen years, Miguel has conceived and directed landmark global campaigns, built lasting brand mythologies, and authored spatial identities for cultural institutions and high-concept luxury houses across London, Paris, Tokyo, and New York. His methodology merges structural structuralism with raw human emotional resonance.
